# Geography: Niphad taluka and the wider Nashik onion cluster

## Niphad taluka

Niphad is described as the most fertile taluka in Nashik district; major crops are grapes, onion, pomegranate, cotton, sugarcane, wheat and maize. Lasalgaon — home to the largest onion market in India and Asia — sits within Niphad taluka.

Real villages within Niphad taluka used in the seed data's farmer/collection-point geography:

- **Lasalgaon** — the market town itself
- **Niphad** — taluka headquarters
- **Pimpalgaon Baswant**
- **Khede** (also spelled Khaede)
- **Datyane**
- **Bhendali**
- **Shivadi**
- **Vinchur** (a second APMC sub-market, "Lasalgaon (Vinchur)")

## Neighboring onion-growing talukas (Nashik district)

Used for additional FPOs/buyers/villages in the extended demo dataset, since a real regional platform would coordinate across more than one taluka:

- **Yeola** — wheat and onion; historically compared to Vidarbha-region agriculture
- **Chandwad**
- **Dindori**
- **Sinnar**

Nashik district has fifteen talukas in total; Niphad, Sinnar, Dindori and Baglan blocks are characterized as more similar to Western Maharashtra agriculture, while Yeola, Nandgaon and Chandwad are described as more like the Vidarbha region.

## Applied in the seed data

The extended seed dataset places its ~18 synthetic farmers across Lasalgaon, Niphad, Pimpalgaon Baswant, Khede, Datyane and Vinchur (all real village names in the real taluka), and introduces a second FPO based in Yeola taluka to demonstrate the platform coordinating across more than one collection centre — while every specific farmer name, phone number and land holding remains fictional.
